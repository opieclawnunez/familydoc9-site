# Launch Package: Telehealth After the Pandemic

Published article: https://docnunez.com/telehealth-after-pandemic.html

## Selection rationale

Chosen from the WordPress modernization candidate report because it ranked highest, is evergreen, supports the Healthcare Innovation content pillar, and has strong relevance for family medicine patients deciding when to use virtual care.

## Editorial review notes

- Removed stale date-specific telehealth policy language.
- Avoided current reimbursement/legal claims because telehealth rules vary by state, payer, and year.
- Kept the advice practical and safety-oriented: use telehealth for focused, stable, non-emergent questions; use in-person/emergency care when symptoms are severe, worsening, or need exam/testing/treatment.
- Included educational-only framing and links to related site articles.
