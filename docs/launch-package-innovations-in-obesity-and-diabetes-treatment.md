## LinkedIn post

Obesity and type 2 diabetes care has changed dramatically over the last few years.

Newer medications, glucose monitoring tools, and procedural options have made treatment more hopeful — but the basics still matter: careful evaluation, realistic goals, nutrition, movement, sleep, medication safety, insurance access, and follow-up.

I published a practical patient-facing guide on what is new in obesity and diabetes treatment, including GLP-1/GIP medications, SGLT2 inhibitors, continuous glucose monitoring, bariatric options, prior authorization, and how to think about treatment as a complete plan rather than a quick fix.
