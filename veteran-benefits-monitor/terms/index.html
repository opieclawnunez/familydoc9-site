This personal monitoring tool is for U.S. Veterans, their families, and caretakers in the Phoenix West Valley area. It is not affiliated with the U.S. Department of Veterans Affairs or any government agency.

Delivery

Updates are delivered to a private Discord channel every 4 days at 8:00 AM MST. The Monitor makes read-only requests to public APIs and websites. No health data is collected, stored, or transmitted.
