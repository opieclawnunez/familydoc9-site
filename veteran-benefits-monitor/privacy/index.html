Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 13, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how the Veteran Benefits Monitor ("we", "us", or "our") handles information when you use our service.

Key principle: We do not and cannot collect, store, or transmit any personal health information. All data we access is publicly available on government websites.

What Information We Collect

We collect only what you provide to us:

  • Email address (optional): If you sign up for email digests, we store your email to deliver the newsletter. You can unsubscribe at any time.
  • Discord user info (optional): If you choose Discord delivery, we store your Discord user ID to send you the digest via bot.

We DO NOT collect:

  • Any VA benefits data, claims information, or health records
  • Any personal identifying information beyond what you voluntarily provide to our sign-up form
  • Browser cookies or tracking data
  • Health data of any kind

What We Do With Information

  • Use your email/Discord ID to deliver your requested digest
  • No marketing, no selling, no sharing with third parties

Your Data & API Keys

The Veteran Benefits Monitor uses the VA Lighthouse Facilities API to retrieve publicly available facility data. Our API key is stored securely in environment variables on our server and is never logged, displayed, or shared. The VA API does not return any personal data — only facility locations, hours, and service information.

Data Security

Sign-up data is stored in a password-protected format. Access requires key-based SSH authentication via Tailscale. No plaintext passwords are ever stored. The server is patched regularly.

Changes to This Policy

We may update this policy. When we do, we'll post the new policy here with an updated "Last updated" date. Significant changes will be noted in the next digest or via the Discord channel.
