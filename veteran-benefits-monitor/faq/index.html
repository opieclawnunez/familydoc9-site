Veteran Benefits Monitor

Frequently Asked Questions

What is the Veteran Benefits Monitor?
An automated personal tool that scans publicly available VA and government data sources for veteran benefits, local deals, and events in the Phoenix West Valley. Results are delivered as a concise digest every 4 days.
Are you affiliated with the VA?
No. The Veteran Benefits Monitor is a personal project built by Dr. Manuel Nunez, a U.S. Veteran and physician in Arizona. We use publicly available data from VA.gov and government APIs.
Do you collect my health data?
No. We only access publicly available facility information, event calendars, and benefit programs. No personal health information is collected, stored, or transmitted.
How is my email used?
Your email is used solely to deliver your requested digest format (email or Discord invite). You can unsubscribe at any time. Your email is never sold, shared, or used for marketing.
How often do you send updates?
Every 4 days at 8:00 AM MST. We balance information freshness with inbox fatigue — daily updates would be overwhelming for most recipients.
What sources do you monitor?
VA.gov Find-Locations, Papago VA Medical Center, Maricopa County Veterans Resources, VA Lighthouse Facilities API, and Luke AFB public communications. If you have a source to suggest, let us know.
Can I request a feature?
Yes — email va-benefits@agentmail.to with your idea. All suggestions are welcome, especially from Veterans who understand the need.
