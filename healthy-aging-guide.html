Topic starter

Healthy aging starts with strength

A practical primer on keeping function, mobility, and confidence over time.

Core message

Strength training is not cosmetic. It supports daily life.

Healthy aging is easier to support when the focus is function: getting up, carrying groceries, walking with confidence, recovering from stress, and staying independent.

Why strength matters

  • Supports muscle and bone health
  • Helps maintain balance and mobility
  • Improves daily capability
  • Can support confidence as people age

How to start

Start small. Use movements you can repeat: sit-to-stands, light resistance, walking, and simple balance work. Keep it sustainable instead of dramatic.
