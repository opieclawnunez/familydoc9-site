Medicine, explained like we're in the same room

I’m originally from New York City, and I tend to speak plainly. Medicine has enough jargon already. You should be able to understand your options without needing a second tab open to translate them.

I’m a retired military veteran and a family medicine physician certified by the American Board of Family Medicine. I see patients at Prosano Health in the Phoenix West Valley. My work spans prevention, everyday health questions, chronic conditions, and the decisions that come up when life doesn't fit into a neat checklist.

The articles here extend the conversations I have in clinic. They are general education, not a diagnosis or a personal treatment plan. You'll find plain-language explanations, practical starting points, and questions to bring to your own clinician.

How I handle what goes on this site

Health information changes. I aim to make claims traceable to reliable sources, distinguish general guidance from my own practical suggestions, and add dates when a piece is published or materially updated. Older posts are being checked in stages; an old posting date is not proof that a page was recently reviewed. If you spot something that needs correction, please say so rather than relying on an article alone to make a care decision.
